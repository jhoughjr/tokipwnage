## Description (4000 max)

**tokipwnage** is the fastest way to explore Toki Pona — the tiny 130-word language designed to simplify thought and spark conversation.

Whether you're learning your first words or reaching for the right one mid-sentence, everything is a tap away.

**A complete dictionary**
Browse every word in the language, each with its meanings grouped by part of speech — nouns, verbs, prepositions, particles, and more. Clean master/detail layout that feels right on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

**Search that keeps up**
Search across words or their meanings and see matches highlighted instantly. Filter the list by part of speech to narrow things down.

**Favorites**
Star the words you use most and jump straight to them. Your favorites stay put across launches.

**Hear it out loud**
Not sure how a word sounds? Speak any word — or a whole phrase — aloud in a voice you choose. Great for building an ear for the language.

**Build phrases**
Type an English sentence and assemble the Toki Pona phrase from matching words, then speak the result. A hands-on way to start *thinking* in Toki Pona.

**Made for Apple platforms**
A single universal app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Native, fast, and offline — the whole dictionary lives on your device, no account or connection required.
