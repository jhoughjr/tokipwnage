Tokipwnage is a Toki Pona dictionary and phrase builder for Mac,
iPhone, and iPad.

Toki Pona is a minimal constructed language of around 130 words. Tokipwnage puts
all of them in one browsable reference and lets you assemble them into phrases.

FEATURES

• Dictionary — every word with its meanings grouped by part of speech, in a
  master/detail view that adapts to iPhone, iPad, and Mac
• Search — across words or their meanings
• Filters and favorites — narrow by part of speech, or to the words you starred;
  favorites persist across launches
• Speech — hear any word or phrase spoken in a voice you choose
• Phrase builder — assemble English into Toki Pona and hear the result

Native SwiftUI on every platform.
